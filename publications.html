The Invisible Editor

Where Does Your Thinking End and the Algorithm Begin?

A book by Md Ataullah Al Mahmud Alif, published by Eliva Press in 2026, that examines how algorithms quietly shape human perception, influence decision-making, and affect independent thought. It explores the boundary between human judgment and machine guidance, the importance of critical thinking, and the need for more responsible AI systems.

Artificial Intelligence and Human Perception: Exploring Bias, Influence and Decision Manipulation

Md Ataullah Al Mahmud Alif's first research paper explores how artificial intelligence can shape human perception, influence biases, and affect decision making. Available as a preprint on SSRN, the research also looks at the ethical questions that arise when AI begins to influence how people think and make judgments.
